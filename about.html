About Me

I am a Computer Science and Engineering student at Metropolitan University. Alongside my studies, I have worked part-time as an Invigilator at Hexas, Zindabazar. This experience helped me develop responsibility, time management, and communication skills.

In addition, I am a She Squad Leader at BDApps, where I work closely with girls to encourage and guide them in the field of technology. I actively inspire and support them to join BDApps and build their skills through creating Lite Apps, Pro Apps, and Android mobile applications. Through this role, I empower young women to explore digital opportunities and build sustainable income streams by creating apps. Even individuals with zero coding knowledge can begin earning through Lite App development.
